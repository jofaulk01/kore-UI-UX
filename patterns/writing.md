# Writing for the interface (microcopy)

Words are half the interface. These rules come from the content guides of Polaris, Atlassian, Material, GOV.UK and Mailchimp.

1. **Buttons are verbs that say what happens:** "Send invoice", "Save changes", "Delete client". Never "Submit", "OK", "Yes".
2. **Sentence case** everywhere.
3. **Use the user's words,** not the system's: "notifications", not "webhook config"; "clients", not "entities".
4. **Short:** cut words that don't change meaning. "Click here to create a new invoice" → "Create invoice".
5. **Same word for the same thing everywhere.** If it's "client" in the nav, it's not "customer" in the table.
6. **Errors explain and fix:** "Enter an amount greater than $0", not "Invalid input".
7. **Success messages name the thing:** "Invoice #1042 sent", not "Success!".
8. **Empty states say what goes here and how to start.**
9. **Numbers and dates readable:** "$4,250.00", "Nov 24", "2 hours ago" (exact time on hover).
10. **No hype words** in the product: avoid "seamlessly", "effortlessly", "supercharge", "unlock", "powered by AI".
