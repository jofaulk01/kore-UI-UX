## Every screen has five states

Design all five, not just the happy one:

1. **Loading:** content on its way.
2. **Empty:** nothing here yet.
3. **Partial:** only a little data (one row; a chart with one point).
4. **Error:** something failed.
5. **Ideal:** full of real data.
