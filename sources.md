Every rule in this library comes from more than one of these. All are free to read. Pages cite them by short name, like **[Carbon]**.

## How disagreements are handled

When sources disagree, the page shows a **Sources disagree** box: who says what, what we follow, and why. Our default leans toward the systems built for business software (Carbon, Atlassian, Polaris, Primer), because that is what you build.
